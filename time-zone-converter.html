How to schedule international meetings painlessly

Instead of doing mental math for Daylight Saving Time across four different countries, use the timeline slider above.

When the text turns Green, it means that specific location is within standard working hours (9:00 AM to 6:00 PM). If you are scheduling a meeting between Sydney, London, and New York, just drag the slider until you find a time where the majority of the pinned cities light up green.
