Common Pitfalls & Engineering Troubleshooting

Multi-Currency Pocket Column Collisions

Revolut statements often list transactions from different currency pockets (EUR, GBP, USD) in a single document. Naively summing amounts without grouping by currency produces meaningless financial totals. TabKit preserves currency codes alongside amounts.

Embedded Currency Exchange (FX) Row Structure

Revolut records currency exchanges as dual-line transactions showing both the sold amount and bought amount. Parsers must link these paired legs to prevent artificial doubling of transaction volumes.

Pending vs Completed Transaction Distinctions

Revolut statements sometimes list pending card pre-authorizations alongside completed transactions. Always verify transaction status flags to avoid recording unsettled authorizations.
