Convert CSV Spreadsheets to Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)

While CSV (Comma-Separated Values) is a ubiquitous plain-text format for data exports, opening CSV files directly in Microsoft Excel can introduce formatting errors, such as stripped leading zeroes on phone numbers, misidentified currency symbols, and unintended date conversions.

This client-side converter converts raw CSV text or files into genuine Microsoft Excel OpenXML workbooks (.xlsx). The file conversion generates a valid multi-column spreadsheet format locally without relying on external cloud APIs. Your sensitive customer records, proprietary sales pipelines, and payroll data remain strictly private on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I convert CSV to XLSX instead of opening CSV directly?

Converting to genuine XLSX preserves exact string data types, prevents Excel from automatically dropping leading zeroes from postal codes or account numbers, and ensures seamless compatibility across Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, and Apple Numbers.

Is my spreadsheet uploaded to any server or third party?

No. All file reading, string parsing, and binary workbook packaging occur entirely in your local browser window. TabKit maintains a zero-telemetry architecture with no backend server uploads.

Does this tool support international CSV delimiters like semicolons?

Yes. The converter automatically detects whether your input file utilizes standard commas, semicolons (common in European regional locales), or tab characters as column separators.
