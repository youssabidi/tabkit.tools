Combine Photos & Scans into a Unified PDF Document

Converting receipt photos, scanned identity documents, whiteboards, and photo sets into a unified, shareable PDF document is essential for administrative, academic, and business filings. Sending multiple loose JPEG or PNG attachments often results in lost documents or out-of-order review.

TabKit Images to PDF compiles multiple JPEG, PNG, and WebP images into a standardized PDF file directly on your device. You can reorder pages, configure margins and orientation, and generate your PDF locally without uploading personal documents to third-party converters.

Our client-side compilation engine preserves high resolution while optimizing page sizing for standard A4 and US Letter document standards. Everything runs locally in browser memory without sending private photos over the network.

How to Combine Multiple Images into a Single PDF

  1. Select or Drop JPG & PNG Images: Load scans, receipts, or design mockups in memory and drag or reorder pages into the desired sequence.
  2. Pick Page Size & Orientation: Choose Fit to Image (preserves native aspect ratio), A4 Portrait, A4 Landscape, or US Letter framing.
  3. Generate & Download PDF Document: Click Generate PDF to assemble crisp multi-page PDF output cleanly in local browser memory.

Aspect Ratio Framing & File Weight Management

Combining scanned receipts or multi-slide deck exports into one PDF prevents scattered email attachments and messy print scaling.

  • Fit to Image vs. Standard Paper: Use Fit to Image for digital screen reading so images are never boxed with uneven white margins; pick A4 or US Letter when preparing documents for physical printing.
  • Normalize Resolution Before Merging: Mixing 4000px smartphone photos with 800px screenshots causes jarring zoom jumps between PDF pages—resize photos uniformly first if needed.
  • Color Profile Preservation: Verify high-contrast text scans remain sharp at 100% zoom prior to submitting official expense or visa paperwork.

Common Pitfalls & Engineering Troubleshooting

Smartphone High-Megapixel File Size Explosion

Converting multiple 12MP or 48MP smartphone photos directly into a PDF can create a massive 80MB document that exceeds corporate email attachment limits. TabKit provides intelligent compression options to maintain readability while keeping file size under 10MB.

Non-Uniform Aspect Ratio Stretching and Distortion

Forcing smartphone photos (4:3 or 16:9) to stretch across fixed A4 dimensions without letterboxing distorts imagery. TabKit centers and scales images proportionally within page boundaries.

CMYK JPEG Color Inversion Artifacts

Photographs exported in print CMYK color mode can render as inverted negatives in web canvas renderers. TabKit normalizes color spaces into standard sRGB before PDF compilation.
