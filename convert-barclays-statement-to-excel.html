Barclays UK Statement Formatting Details

Barclays statements typically format dates as DD MMM (for example, 14 Oct) and separate Money Out from Money In.

  • UK DD MMM Date Format: When sorting across calendar year boundaries, append the statement year before converting date strings into Excel Date serials.

  • Balance Forward Rows: Remove "Start balance" and "Carried forward" rows prior to running SUM formulas across transaction columns.
