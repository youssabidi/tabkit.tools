Why Merge PDFs Locally?

Most commercial online PDF utilities upload your documents to third-party cloud servers to perform merging operations. If you are handling sensitive documents such as tax returns, NDAs, medical records, or proprietary corporate agreements, uploading them to unknown cloud servers creates serious privacy and compliance risks.

TabKit Tools PDF Merger executes entirely inside your browser using client-side WebAssembly and JavaScript binary streams. There are zero file size caps, zero queues, and zero bytes transferred across the internet.
