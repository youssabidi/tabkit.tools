Why Convert Excel to CSV Locally?

CSV (Comma-Separated Values) is the universal language for databases, SQL imports, analytics scripts, and data engineering pipelines. However, uploading proprietary spreadsheets or customer data to third-party online converters exposes confidential business numbers to remote servers.

TabKit Tools Excel to CSV Converter runs entirely in your local browser sandbox using SheetJS. No workbook bytes ever travel across the network.
