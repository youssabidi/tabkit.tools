Understanding WCAG 2.1 Color Contrast Standards

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1) specify relative luminance ratios between text and background colors to ensure digital products are accessible to users with low vision or color perception deficiencies:

  • Level AA (Minimum): Requires a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal body text (under 18pt regular or 14pt bold) and at least 3.0:1 for large text and interactive UI borders.
  • Level AAA (Enhanced): Requires a contrast ratio of at least 7.0:1 for normal text and 4.5:1 for large text headings.
