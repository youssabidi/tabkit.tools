Add Custom Page Numbers and Footers to PDF Documents

Assembling multi-page reports, legal briefs, and business proposals requires standardized pagination for easy referencing. Adding page numbers across merged PDF files ensures professional document presentation and effortless navigation.

TabKit PDF Page Numberer stamps clean page numbering onto any PDF document. Configure starting numbers, choose positioning (bottom-center, bottom-right, header), customize formats (e.g. "Page X of Y"), and download the paginated PDF instantly without uploading files to remote servers.

The vector stamping engine integrates seamlessly with existing margins, producing crisp numerical pagination readable on digital screens and high-resolution printouts alike.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I exclude the cover page from page numbering?

Yes. You can configure the tool to start numbering from page 2, leaving your title or cover page clean.

What numbering formats are supported?

You can choose simple numbers (1, 2, 3), "Page X", or full pagination ("Page X of Y") in various font sizes and alignments.

Is my PDF document kept private?

Yes. All vector text stamping and binary recompilation occur directly in your browser. No files are uploaded to any server.
