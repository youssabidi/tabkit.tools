Add Custom Page Numbers and Footers to PDF Documents

Assembling multi-page reports, legal briefs, and business proposals requires standardized pagination for easy referencing. Adding page numbers across merged PDF files ensures professional document presentation and effortless navigation.

TabKit PDF Page Numberer stamps clean page numbering onto any PDF document. Configure starting numbers, choose positioning (bottom-center, bottom-right, header), customize formats (e.g. "Page X of Y"), and download the paginated PDF instantly without uploading files to remote servers.

The vector stamping engine integrates seamlessly with existing margins, producing crisp numerical pagination readable on digital screens and high-resolution printouts alike.

How to Stamp Sequential Page Numbers onto Any PDF

  1. Load Your PDF File: Choose the document requiring page stamps—such as legal briefs, handbooks, or printed report packets.
  2. Choose Format & Corner Placement: Pick "Page {n} of {total}", "{n} / {total}", or "{n}", select alignment (Bottom Center, Bottom Right, etc.), and skip cover page 1 if desired.
  3. Apply & Download Numbered PDF: Render crisp embedded vector typography directly onto each page and save the numbered document immediately.

Typography Margins & Cover-Page Pagination Tips

Consistent page numbering is mandatory for court exhibits, bound training packets, and multi-chapter PDF submissions.

  • Checking Existing Footer Overlap: Preview margins if your original PDF already places dense footnotes or company footers near the bottom 25pt edge.
  • Rotated & Landscape Pages: Check documents mixing portrait and landscape exhibits so stamped coordinates land cleanly within each page’s visible trim box.
  • Font Embedding Size Impact: Using standard PDF Type-1 Helvetica metrics stamps crisp crisp labels across hundreds of pages while adding only a few kilobytes to file size.
  • Bilingual & Roman Numeral Prefaces: For documents with roman-numeral introductions, begin numbering from the first Arabic page and skip introductory pages to maintain professional publishing standards.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I exclude the cover page from page numbering?

Yes. You can configure the tool to start numbering from page 2, leaving your title or cover page clean.

What numbering formats are supported?

You can choose simple numbers (1, 2, 3), "Page X", or full pagination ("Page X of Y") in various font sizes and alignments.

Is my PDF document kept private?

Yes. All vector text stamping and binary recompilation occur directly in your browser. No files are uploaded to any server.

Common Pitfalls & Engineering Troubleshooting

Page Number Collisions with Existing Document Content

Stamping page numbers too close to the page margin can cause numbers to overlap existing footer text or charts. TabKit provides adjustable margin padding controls to ensure clean placement.

Unwanted Page Numbering on Title and Cover Pages

Displaying "Page 1" on an executive proposal cover page looks unprofessional. TabKit includes a toggle to suppress numbering on the first page while maintaining accurate overall page counts.

Font Embedding Failures Causing Glyph Scrambling

Stamping text without embedding standard font metrics can cause page numbers to render with fallback fonts on systems lacking specific fonts. TabKit embeds standard PDF font subsets.
