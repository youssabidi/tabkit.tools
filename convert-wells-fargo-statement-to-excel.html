Extracting Wells Fargo Transactions Locally

How do I export Wells Fargo PDF statements to Excel safely? You can convert your Wells Fargo PDF statements directly inside your web browser using TabKit Tools. The system parses PDF text streams, groups deposits and withdrawals into individual columns, and produces a native Excel .xlsx workbook with zero data sent across the web.

Wells Fargo statements contain separate transaction tables for electronic deposits, checks paid, debit card purchases, and recurring monthly fees. Reconciling these activities manually in a spreadsheet is time-consuming and error-prone, especially for businesses with high transaction volumes.

TabKit Tools reads the spatial text layout of your Wells Fargo PDF, identifies dates and descriptions, and places debits and credits into structured columns. Because the parsing engine runs entirely in JavaScript on your computer, your banking credentials and financial history remain 100% confidential.
