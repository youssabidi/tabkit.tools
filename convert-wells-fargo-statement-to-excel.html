Wells Fargo Transaction Table Quirks

Wells Fargo combines posting date, check number, description, additions, subtractions, and ending daily balance into dense multi-column layouts.

  • Separate Additions & Subtractions: Because inflows and outflows sit in adjacent columns, check column sign alignment before summing net cash movement.
  • Check Reference Numbers: Cleared check numbers often appear right after the posting date—keep them in a dedicated column when auditing vendor payments.
  • Fee Rebate Lines: Monthly service fee waivers appear as offsetting credits; group by description to verify net bank charges.
