TabKit Open Development & Community Feedback

TabKit Tools is built for power users, developers, analysts, and privacy advocates who believe in fast, zero-telemetry, client-side software. Our roadmap is driven directly by community feedback, bug reports, and workflow suggestions from real-world usage.

If you encounter unexpected behavior, edge-case parsing errors, or rendering issues on a particular browser or operating system, submitting a report helps us patch issues quickly. We also actively welcome requests for new browser-based utilities that can be implemented with local-first, client-side web technologies.

Frequently Asked Questions

What details should I include in a bug report?

Providing your browser name and version, operating system (Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android), the tool name, and the specific steps or sample text that triggered the error helps us reproduce and resolve the issue immediately.

Can I request a brand new offline productivity tool?

Yes! If there is a calculation, conversion, or formatting workflow you find yourself performing frequently that could run privately in a browser, submit a Tool Request through this form.

How is my feedback submitted without tracking?

The feedback form formats your submission cleanly into a standardized markdown template that you can copy or send directly via email, ensuring no third-party tracking scripts monitor your actions.
