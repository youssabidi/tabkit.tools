Split PDF Pages and Extract Custom Page Ranges

Large PDF files containing multiple chapters, invoices, or legal filings often need to be separated into distinct documents or specific single-page extracts for email attachments and secure distribution.

TabKit PDF Splitter extracts pages and custom ranges (e.g. 1-3, 5, 8-12) locally. The tool reconstructs valid standalone PDF documents with high fidelity in your browser session with zero server uploads and total privacy.

Extracting pages directly in browser memory eliminates the risk of confidential business agreements, financial statements, or patient medical histories being logged on external cloud servers.

How to Split PDFs or Extract Selected Pages

  1. Drop a Multi-Page PDF: Load the source PDF to inspect total page count and select individual pages or ranges cleanly.
  2. Select Pages to Keep (or Invert): Click thumbnails/indices—or use Invert and All shortcuts—to pick only the pages you want in the extracted file.
  3. Click Extract & Save: Compile a lightweight standalone PDF containing strictly your chosen pages in preserved sequence.

Pruning Heavy Reports & Isolating Exhibits

Splitting massive compliance packets or 200-page manuals into focused sections avoids email attachment caps and keeps confidential appendices out of circulation.

  • Invert Selection for Quick Page Deletion: When removing 2 unwanted cover or appendix pages from a 40-page packet, select those 2 pages and click Invert to keep the remaining 38 instantly.
  • Lossless Asset Stripping: Extracted PDFs copy only the fonts and images referenced on your kept pages, shrinking bloated multi-section downloads automatically.
  • Preserved Text & Links: Original selectable text, crisp vectors, and form field visuals remain sharp in the extracted subset.
  • Zero Recompression Quality: Page extraction reorganizes the internal PDF cross-reference table and page dictionary directly; scanned photo pages and embedded fonts are copied verbatim with 0% re-encoding loss.
  • Bookmark & Link Integrity: When splitting chapters, internal page links within the extracted range continue to function correctly in standard PDF readers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I extract non-consecutive pages?

Yes. You can specify custom page ranges such as "1, 3-5, 9" to extract exactly the pages you need into a new document.

Does splitting a PDF reduce document quality?

No. PDF pages are extracted losslessly, preserving original vector fonts, high-resolution imagery, and document layout.

Are my sensitive PDF documents uploaded to external servers?

No. All parsing and binary extraction execute 100% locally in your web browser memory.
