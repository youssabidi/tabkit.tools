Why Extract PDF Pages Locally?

Standard online PDF splitters process your files on remote web servers, creating data privacy hazards when handling sensitive contracts, bank statements, or personal records.

TabKit PDF Splitter extracts and repackages only the pages you specify entirely in your browser using local binary operations. No file ever touches an external server.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I specify which pages to extract?

Enter single page numbers or ranges separated by commas, such as 1-3, 5, 8-12, to download a new document containing only those pages.

Is my original PDF modified or overwritten?

No. Your original file remains untouched on your disk. A brand new PDF containing only your selected pages is generated and downloaded to your device.
