Simple by design

One clean tab for your quick daily tasks.

Ever notice how doing simple things online—like cleaning messy links, jotting a quick note, or formatting text—forces you to open 5 different websites covered in popup ads and signup forms? TabKit puts those small tools together in one calm, quiet tab.

✓

No accounts, no email required

You don't need to sign up, create a password, or verify an email address to use anything here. Open the tab, get your task done, and move on with your day.

🔒

Your work stays on your computer

Whatever you write, paste, or clean stays inside your browser. We don't save your text to any server or track what you are working on.

⚡

Customize your own desk

Pick only the tools you actually use and pin them to your screen. When you close your browser and come back tomorrow, your notes and tools are right where you left them.

How to get the most out of it

Pin tabkit.tools as your first browser tab at work or home. Whenever you need to take a quick scratchpad note, strip messy tracking codes off a link, or compare two versions of text, it's just one click away.
