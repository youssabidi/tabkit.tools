JSON Web Token (JWT) Decoder

Decode and inspect JWT header and payload claims securely in your browser with expiration validation.

Why should you never paste production JWTs into typical websites?

Production JWT tokens often carry confidential user IDs, scopes, email addresses, and session permissions. Third-party decoders can intercept or log these tokens. TabKit decodes Base64URL strictly in client memory without external requests.

Privacy & Local-First Philosophy

TabKit Tools executes every transformation strictly in client memory. By executing on your device, you get zero latency, complete protection against data breaches, and full offline availability.
