View and Edit PDF Document Properties & Metadata

PDF documents contain embedded metadata properties including Title, Author, Subject, Keywords, Creator application, and Producer tool. Before publishing research papers or legal filings, editing or stripping these properties is critical for privacy, compliance, and professional formatting.

TabKit PDF Metadata Editor reads and updates document info dictionaries locally. Modify title tags, update author credits, or clear legacy metadata completely without uploading sensitive PDF files to external servers.

By inspecting and updating metadata before distribution, you eliminate hidden author paths, software versions, and internal network usernames embedded in files by word processors.

Frequently Asked Questions

What metadata fields can I edit in my PDF?

You can edit the Title, Author, Subject, Keywords, and Creator fields, or wipe all fields to anonymize the document.

Will editing metadata modify the page content or text formatting?

No. Only the document information dictionary and XMP metadata stream are modified. Page contents and formatting remain unchanged.

Are my confidential PDF documents uploaded to external servers?

No. All reading and writing of PDF binary streams execute 100% locally on your computer.
