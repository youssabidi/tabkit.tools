PDF Page Rotator

Quickly fix upside-down or sideways scanned documents. Permanently rotate all pages, odd/even pages, or specific page numbers with zero server latency.

Permanently Correct PDF Orientation

When paper documents are fed through physical sheet-feed scanners, it is common for pages to end up sideways or upside-down. Merely rotating the view in your PDF reader does not save the underlying coordinate transform, meaning anyone opening the PDF later will still see the disoriented pages.

TabKit Tools PDF Rotator modifies the native internal page dictionary rotation matrix (/Rotate 90, 180, 270), ensuring the fix is permanent across all PDF readers, mobile phones, and printing software.

Targeted Page Scopes

Rotate all pages at once, or selectively rotate odd, even, or specific page ranges (e.g. 1, 3-5).

Zero File Re-Compression

No quality degradation or loss of OCR text layers. Original streams are retained 1:1.
