PDF Page Rotator

Quickly fix upside-down or sideways scanned documents. Permanently rotate all pages, odd/even pages, or specific page numbers with zero server latency.

Rotate PDF Pages Permanently (90, 180, 270 Degrees)

Document scanners and mobile camera apps frequently produce upside-down or sideways pages. Rotating pages to proper reading orientation is essential before emailing contracts, sharing architectural drawings, or archiving corporate records.

TabKit PDF Rotator rotates individual pages or entire documents permanently. Adjust page orientation clockwise or counterclockwise and save your re-oriented document locally with zero server uploads and total privacy.

Unlike online PDF services that convert your document to raster images during rotation, TabKit modifies the native rotation flag within the PDF dictionary, preserving 100% of the original text searchability and vector fidelity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is page rotation permanent when downloaded?

Yes. The rotation updates the internal PDF page dictionary, ensuring correct orientation in Adobe Acrobat, Apple Preview, and mobile viewers.

Can I rotate only specific pages in a document?

Yes. You can rotate individual sideways pages without modifying the rest of your document.

Are my confidential PDF documents safe?

Yes. Everything executes strictly on your device CPU. No files are uploaded to external servers.
