Encrypt and Password-Protect PDF Documents Privately

Confidential legal contracts, intellectual property documentation, financial statements, and personal identity files require strict access controls. Uploading sensitive PDF documents to free online file converters creates serious data breach risks and privacy non-compliance.

TabKit secures your files by applying industry-standard 128-bit and 256-bit AES encryption directly in your browser. The cryptographic keys and document streams are processed locally using client-side WebAssembly and JavaScript. Your documents and passwords never touch a remote server, ensuring complete compliance with confidentiality and data protection standards.

How to Lock & Encrypt PDF Files with a Password

  1. Drop the Unencrypted PDF: Load tax filings, HR contracts, payroll summaries, or private scan packets directly into browser memory.
  2. Enter a Strong Open Password: Specify the user password required to open the PDF (click Show to verify spelling before locking).
  3. Click Lock & Download PDF: Encrypt the file cleanly inside your browser and verify opening the downloaded PDF prompts for your chosen password.

PDF Encryption Scope & Best Sharing Practices

Sending sensitive contracts or tax forms over email without file-level encryption leaves attachments readable anywhere a mailbox is synced.

  • Share Passwords Out-of-Band: Never send the PDF password in the same email body as the encrypted PDF; send the password via SMS, Signal, or phone call instead.
  • Strong Passphrases Beat Short PINs: Short numeric passwords can be cracked rapidly against offline PDF files—aim for 12+ random characters or a 4+ word passphrase.
  • Verify Viewer Compatibility: Test opening the locked PDF in Chrome, macOS Preview, or Adobe Acrobat before emailing time-sensitive client documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

What encryption standard is applied to the PDF?

Documents are encrypted using standard PDF security specifications compatible with modern viewers, restricting unauthorized reading, text copying, and document modifications.

Can TabKit recover my password if I forget it?

No. Because TabKit has zero access to your device and never stores passwords on a server, forgotten passwords cannot be recovered. Be sure to keep a secure backup of your password.

Is the encrypted PDF compatible with Adobe Acrobat and mobile readers?

Yes. The generated files adhere strictly to standard PDF specifications and open seamlessly in Adobe Acrobat, Apple Preview, Google Chrome, and iOS/Android PDF readers.
