Chase Statement Format Considerations

Chase PDFs often separate Deposits and Additions, ATM & Debit Card Withdrawals, and Electronic Withdrawals into distinct sub-tables within a single statement.

  • Section Headers & Subtotals: Skip summary balance-forward lines if you only want raw ledger entries imported into a pivot table or cash-flow sheet.
  • MM/DD Date Rollovers: December-to-January billing cycles use month/day markers per line; confirm the statement year column when sorting multi-month archives.
  • Account Number Exclusion: Page footers and account masks are excluded from transaction row exports so shared workbooks stay concise.
